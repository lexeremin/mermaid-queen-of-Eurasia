# Product Overview

## Vision
"Mermaid Queen of Eurasia" is a browser-based 3D low-poly action RPG with a fixed top-down camera. Rosa, a fictional mermaid queen, arrives in a surreal fantasy Russia and builds a kingdom by persuading lonely, confused or unhappy NPCs with her Mermaid Aura, and by fighting absurd bureaucratic monsters.

## Audience
Casual-to-core players on desktop and iPhone Safari who enjoy short, atmospheric, humorous RPG sessions. No install, no login.

## Tone
Surreal, romantic, darkly humorous, absurd. Rosa's exaggerated fantasy, not a realistic political story.

Hard guardrails:
- Corrupt politicians and bureaucratic monsters are fictional. They must not resemble or represent real people.
- The game is not autobiographical and depicts no real people.
- No copying of Stardew Valley or Diablo II assets, characters, maps, UI, music or artwork.

## MVP: vertical slice
One village (8 NPCs, shops, small house, river, forest entrance, quest board), one forest (herbs, pearls, monsters, hidden shrine), one dungeon (3 rooms, loot, boss arena). The player can: start, move, talk, accept a quest, enter the forest, fight, loot, enter the dungeon, beat the boss, unlock a transformation, save and reload.

## Success criteria
- The slice is completable in roughly 20–40 minutes.
- Runs on desktop and iPhone Safari within the performance budgets.
- Works offline; stats sync when online.
- Looks like one coherent game (single palette, consistent asset style).

## Out of scope (slice)
Multiplayer, login/accounts, purchases, extra regions, extra languages, leaderboards and cross-device restore (stretch).

# Product Overview

## Vision
"Mermaid Queen of Eurasia" is a browser-based 3D low-poly action RPG with a fixed top-down camera. Rosa, a rose-crowned woman who is secretly a mermaid queen, arrives in a surreal, snowy Red Square and builds a kingdom by mesmerizing lonely, confused or unhappy Russian men with her Mermaid Aura, and by fighting absurd bureaucratic monsters. She can transform into a mermaid and use the Tidal Song skill.

## Audience
Casual-to-core players on desktop and iPhone Safari who enjoy short, atmospheric, humorous RPG sessions. No install, no login.

## Tone
Surreal, romantic, darkly humorous, absurd. Rosa's exaggerated fantasy, not a realistic political story.

Hard guardrails (details in `docs/likeness-and-consent.md`):
- Only the hero is based on a real person (stylized, with consent). No other real person is depicted.
- Corrupt politicians and bureaucratic monsters are fictional. They must not resemble or represent real people.
- No real political figures, state emblems, flags or party symbols; landmarks are stylized and surreal.
- No copying of Stardew Valley or Diablo II assets, characters, maps, UI, music or artwork.

## MVP: vertical slice
One Red Square district (8 NPCs, market stalls, Rosa's small house, river, garden entrance, quest board), one snowy garden (herbs, pearls, monsters, hidden shrine), one underground dungeon (3 rooms, loot, boss arena). The player can: start, move, talk, accept a quest, enter the garden, fight, loot, enter the dungeon, beat the boss, unlock the mermaid transformation, save and reload.

## Success criteria
- The slice is completable in roughly 20–40 minutes.
- Runs on desktop and iPhone Safari within the performance budgets.
- Works offline; stats sync when online.
- Looks like one coherent game (single palette, consistent asset style).

## Out of scope (slice)
Multiplayer, login/accounts, purchases, extra regions, extra languages, leaderboards and cross-device restore (stretch).

# Likeness and Consent

## Decision (2026-09-24)
The hero, Rosa, is a **stylized low-poly character based on the look of a real person** who has agreed to it. The user confirmed in chat that the person in the reference photo is the user or someone who agreed.

## Rules
- **Stylized only.** The model captures styling (hair, roses, ivory jacket, petals, skin tone, overall silhouette). No face scan, no photo-derived textures, no image-to-3D of the face. Palette-atlas colors only.
- **The reference photo is never committed.** It lives in `assets-src/reference/` (git-ignored) and nowhere in the repo, docs or CI. Do not paste it into docs, issues or PRs.
- **The hero, and Prince Sasha.** The hero is based on the styling of a consenting real person (above). Since 2026-09-25 Prince Sasha's face and hair are also a stylized low-poly likeness of the project owner, made at their request from a photo they supplied (swept brown hair, light eyes, straight brows, light stubble). No scan and no photo-derived texture; the photo is not stored in the repository. No other real person is depicted. All other NPCs are original archetypes.
- **Politics.** No real political figures. Corrupt politicians and bureaucratic monsters are fictional and must not resemble real politicians. No state emblems, flags or party symbols; no Lenin Mausoleum; tower crowns are a fantasy ruby-crystal spire.
- **Enemies.** The satirical politician enemies (display names Uncle Sosunok, Zelebeba and Ugrumiy Putan since 2026-09-25; earlier Gavel Speaker, Golden-Pen Tycoon and Megaphone Demagogue) are original caricatures defined by costume and weapon. On 2026-09-24 a request to model enemies on specific real, living political leaders was declined and replaced by these archetypes. The renames only change display names: the designs stay original and must not be reworked toward any real person's face or likeness.
- **Withdrawal.** If the person asks to be removed, replace the hero design with an original one; the stylized model is a design asset and can be redrawn.

## Look notes derived from the reference (no identification)
Dark hair in an updo with a top bun and soft wavy strands; a cluster of cream, blush and lilac-pearl roses on one side of the head; warm light skin; dark almond eyes; nude-pink lips; ivory tweed jacket with a wide boat neckline; loose white petals drifting around the shoulders.

## Later content (bosses, people, the archangels)
- Bosses and generic NPCs are original satirical archetypes defined by costume and props (for example a lobbyist, a sanctions baron, a talk-show host). They never carry a real person's name, face, party or national symbol, and they never mock a nationality or an ethnic group; the joke is about corruption and bureaucracy.
- The three archangels (Michael, Gabriel, Serafima) are fictional small winged children of Rosa and Prince Sasha. They are not modelled on any real child.

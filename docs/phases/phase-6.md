# Phase 6 — Red Square map

**Status:** ✅ Complete

## Goal
Replace the winter village with a stylized, surreal Red Square district: a walkable, collidable, data-driven map built from the existing systems (collision, zones, instancing) plus new landmark assets. The hero and pipeline come from Phases 3–5; NPCs are Phase 7.

## Layout (world units, +x east, +z south, camera looks north)
Camera sits south of the player, so tall things go **north** of the player (they never hide Rosa) and low things go south.
- **North end:** St. Basil's-style cathedral (`lmk_basil`) centered at (0, −31.5), beyond the walkable bounds as the skyline.
- **West side (x = −19):** Kremlin-style wall (crenellated segments, 6 m each) with a clock-tower gate at z = −4 and small corner towers. Towers end in a fantasy ruby-crystal spire (no state star).
- **East side (x = +21):** GUM-style arcade facade (8 m segments): lit windows, string lights, glass roof.
- **South end:** red-brick museum with two tent-roofed towers at z = 33.5, the backdrop for the spawn (0, 20).
- **Frozen canal** across the plaza at z ≈ −11 with a stone arch bridge at x = 0. South of it: Rosa's lodge, festive market stalls, quest board. North of it: the cathedral forecourt.
- **Alexander Garden gate** (brick arch with a fantasy spire) on the west edge at (−14.5, 14); zone `garden-entrance`.
- Cobblestone plaza, ornate lampposts, fir trees in tubs, perimeter forest beyond the walls (visual only).
- Bounds x ±16.5, z −24…28.

Real-landmark liberties (documented in `docs/likeness-and-consent.md`): orientation is flipped and compressed, the canal is invented, no Mausoleum, no state emblems.

## Scope
1. **Assets** (`tools/blender/build_redsquare_assets.py`): `lmk_basil`, `bld_kremlin_wall`, `bld_kremlin_tower`, `bld_gum`, `bld_museum`, `bld_arch_bridge`, `bld_garden_gate`, `prop_lamppost`, `prop_fir_tub`. New budget class **`lmk`** (≤ 4 000 tris, ≤ 300 KB, ≤ 128 px).
2. **Cobblestone tile:** `public/assets/atlas/cobble_tile.png`, a 32×32 palette-quantized tiling texture generated by `tools/make-tiles.py` (standard library only), used on the plaza ground with nearest filtering. First tiling detail texture; resolves the Phase 3 open item.
3. **Retire** village-only assets: izba, plank bridge, old gate, well, fence, logs, lantern. GLBs, manifest entries and builders are removed; the ledger keeps a "Retired" list. Kept: shops, hut (Rosa's lodge, the brief requires a small house), barrel, crate, questboard, spruce, birch.
4. **Map data** `src/data/maps/red-square.ts`; `MapData` gains `plaza` (replaces `square`); `paths` may be empty. `Village.tsx` becomes a generic `MapScene.tsx`.
5. **Tests:** `red-square.test.ts` (spawn free, bounds, flood fill reaches the garden gate and the forecourt only via the bridge, canal and wall block).
6. Docs: asset pipeline (lmk class, cobble tile), ledger, features, this file.

## Result
- **Map** `src/data/maps/red-square.ts`: bounds x ±16.5 / z −24…28, spawn (0, 20); backdrop (cathedral, 12 wall segments, 3 towers, 8 arcade segments, museum) sits outside the bounds with `collide: false`; plaza has the arch bridge over a frozen canal (z ≈ −11), Rosa's lodge, 7 market stalls, quest board, barrels, crates, 11 lampposts, 7 fir tubs, the Alexander Garden gate; 170 backdrop trees.
- **Collision** unchanged systems; new footprints in the manifest. Filler boxes seal the canal at the bridge like before.
- **Assets:** 9 new (cathedral 1 758 tris, wall 288, tower 444, arcade 564, museum 614, bridge 324, garden gate 464, lamppost 134, fir tub 270), all in budget; new `lmk` class. 7 village assets retired (izba, plank bridge, village gate, well, fence, logs, lantern); ledger has a Retired section. Kept: shops, hut (Rosa's lodge), quest board, barrel, crate, spruce, birch.
- **Cobble tile** `tools/make-tiles.py` → `cobble_tile.png` (145 bytes), plaza ground via `MapScene` (`CobblePlaza`), 2 m per tile.
- **Tests:** `red-square.test.ts` (spawn free, bounds, backdrop outside bounds, blocking of canal/stall/lodge/gate posts/lamppost, flood fill reaches the garden gate and the forecourt only across the bridge). 55 tests in total.
- `Village.tsx` → generic `MapScene.tsx`; `square` in `MapData` → `plaza`.

## Verification (browser)
- Overview (`?zoom=3`): plaza, cathedral, wall + clock tower, arcade, canal + bridge, stalls, lodge and museum all render; 29 draw calls, 45.5k triangles, about 100 fps.
- Scripted teleport + key runs: crosses the bridge to the north bound (z −23.6); blocked at the canal beside the bridge (z −8.66), at a stall (x 11.05), gate post (x −13.55), lodge (z 20.9), lamppost (z 11.32), barrel, and the east/south map edges; walks between the gate posts into zone `garden-entrance` (HUD shows "Alexander Garden").
- 375×812: joystick drives Rosa toward the bridge, walk clip plays, 25 draw calls, no console errors, no page scroll.
- Cobble tile was tuned after the first look (stones halved to 0.5 m, lighter mix).

## Known issues / notes for later phases
- The Kremlin wall, arcade and museum are backdrop only: the wall and arcade are decorative (no entrances). Interiors are out of scope.
- The tall clock tower and cathedral only occlude Rosa near the map edges; occlusion fade stays in Phase 15.
- The cathedral silhouette is orientation-flipped and simplified on purpose; no real building plans were used.
- Canal, bridge and Tidal Song: the mermaid form will cross the canal in the Transformations phase.
- Not measured on a physical iPhone (Phase 16).

## Out of scope
NPCs, shops UI, quest interaction, garden transition, occlusion fade, dynamic lights, water animation.
